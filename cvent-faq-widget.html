FAQ

Where is the venue located?
The Future Tech Summit 2024 will be held at the Grand Convention Center, 123 Innovation Blvd, San Francisco, CA. It is accessible via BART and Muni.
Is the venue wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the entire venue is fully WCAG and ADA compliant, featuring ramps, elevators, and accessible restrooms on every floor. We also have designated seating areas.
Can I get a refund if I can't attend?
Tickets are refundable up to 30 days before the event. After that, you may transfer your ticket to another attendee by contacting support@futuretech.com.
Are meals included?
Standard and VIP tickets include daily lunch and coffee breaks. Student tickets include access to coffee breaks but lunch must be purchased separately.
