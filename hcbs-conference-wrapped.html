Export Guide

To export as PNG images:

1. Navigate to each frame using the arrow buttons

2. Use your browser's screenshot tool or press Cmd+Shift+4 (Mac) or Win+Shift+S (Windows)

3. For best quality, zoom your browser to 100% (Cmd+0 or Ctrl+0)

To create MP4 video:

1. Use screen recording software (OBS, QuickTime, or browser extension)

2. Click Play to auto-advance through all frames

3. Each frame displays for 4.5 seconds

4. Export recording at 1080x1920 resolution

For high-quality PNG export:

Use Puppeteer or html2canvas library for programmatic screenshot capture at exact 1080x1920 dimensions.
